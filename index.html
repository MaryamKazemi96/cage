Human-Robot Coexistence Dataset

Multimodal recordings of humans and mobile robots sharing dynamic environments.

About the Dataset

The Human-Robot Coexistence Dataset contains multimodal recordings of humans and robots sharing dynamic environments. It is intended to enable research on human-robot interaction, navigation, coordination, intent communication, and social navigation with consistent experimental context.

Experimental Scenarios

Free-space human movement

Humans move naturally in open space while robots operate in the same environment.

Human movement with obstacles

Human trajectories are recorded under obstacle constraints with concurrent robot activity.

Two-robot interaction

Two mobile robots coordinate motion and conflict resolution in shared trajectories.

Two-robot interaction with projected robot goals / intent communication

Ground-projected cues communicate robot intent to improve predictability and coexistence.

Dataset Contents

  • ROS 2 bags synchronized experiment recordings from robot systems
  • OptiTrack trajectories motion capture trajectories for humans and robots
  • Human goals task goals and intent annotations
  • Survey data participant responses and subjective measurements
  • Experimental metadata scenario labels, timestamps, and trial descriptors
  • Toolkit dataset loading, visualization, and analysis utilities

Intent Communication

A dedicated scenario in this dataset evaluates ground-projected robot goals as an intent communication mechanism. These visual projections are used to make robot plans legible to nearby humans, enabling controlled studies of predictability, coordination, and social navigation behavior.
